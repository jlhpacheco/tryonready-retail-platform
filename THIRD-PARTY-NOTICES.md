## Demo assets

No model, customer, boutique, garment, logo, font, icon, music, or video asset is approved merely because it appears online.

For every demo asset, record:

- File or asset name
- Source
- Owner
- License or written permission
- Permitted uses
- Required attribution
- Expiration or restrictions

## Prohibited assumptions

- Search-engine results are not a license.
- Social-media availability is not permission.
- A product listing does not authorize reuse of its photographs.
- AI generation does not automatically eliminate third-party rights concerns.
