# Demo Script

Target length: 2 minutes 30 seconds to 3 minutes.

## 0:00–0:20 — The problem

Introduce Elena, owner of the fictional women’s boutique Luna & Thread. She photographs new arrivals on her phone, works with a small team, and cannot afford an enterprise virtual try-on integration.

Message:

> TryOnReady gives independent boutiques a no-code path from a phone photo to a customer-ready virtual try-on experience.

## 0:20–0:45 — Mobile boutique onboarding

Show the phone-sized PWA.

- Elena opens the boutique application.
- She enters a short fictional business profile.
- The application is approved for the demo.

## 0:45–1:15 — Garment readiness

- Elena adds the Blue Linen Blazer.
- The first source image receives actionable readiness feedback.
- She replaces it with a better authorized demo image.
- Product fields become complete.

Explain that the score measures digital-asset readiness, not physical fit.

## 1:15–1:45 — Real YouCam integration

- Select `Validate with YouCam`.
- Show the real task lifecycle: submitted, processing, completed.
- Display the generated apparel virtual try-on result.
- Briefly identify the YouCam Apparel VTO API as the core external capability.

## 1:45–2:10 — Review and publication

- An administrator reviews the source asset and result.
- Approve the product.
- Generate a public product page and QR/share link.

## 2:10–2:35 — Consumer experience

- A consumer opens the product page on a phone.
- Show the consent notice.
- Upload an authorized demonstration image.
- Display the private virtual try-on result.

State that the boutique cannot view the customer photograph.

## 2:35–2:55 — Boutique value

Return to Elena’s dashboard:

- Virtual try-on activity
- Product-link clicks
- API usage
- Products needing better images

Use clearly labeled synthetic metrics.

## 2:55–3:00 — Closing

> TryOnReady brings advanced virtual try-on to the boutiques that enterprise retail technology often leaves behind.

## Video restrictions

- No real customer or boutique information
- No credentials or private configuration
- No unlicensed music, images, logos, or trademarks
- No claims that a generated image guarantees physical fit
- The video must show the working product on its intended device size
