# Demo Script

All people, businesses, products, and identifiers in this script are fictional.

## Persona

Elena Rivera operates Luna & Thread, a small independent women's clothing boutique with three employees.

## First-phase walkthrough

1. Open the public landing page on a mobile-sized screen.
2. Introduce TryOnReady: “Virtual try-on for boutiques without the enterprise budget.”
3. Show the four workflow links and explain that Product Readiness works now while the other three pages are transparent scaffold previews.
4. Open Boutique Application and describe a future mobile-friendly onboarding flow.
5. Open Product Readiness, enter the synthetic blazer details, choose a synthetic image, and run the readiness check. Show the returned image-quality guidance.
6. Explain that the provider boundary is currently disabled: no photograph is uploaded and no YouCam request is made.
7. Open Admin Review and Consumer Try-On to show the planned approval and publishing boundaries.
8. Call the API health endpoint and OpenAPI document to demonstrate the buildable backend foundation.

## Future reviewed demo

After live integration is separately approved, Elena will photograph a synthetic blazer, correct readiness problems, validate it through Apparel VTO, and publish a customer-ready preview. That behavior is not part of this scaffold.
