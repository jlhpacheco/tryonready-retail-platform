# Product Scope

## Product statement

TryOnReady gives independent clothing boutiques an affordable, no-code path from a phone photograph to a customer-ready virtual try-on experience.

## Primary audience

Small, owner-operated clothing boutiques, especially women-owned and women-operated stores with tight margins, limited staffing, and no internal development team.

## Demo persona

**Elena Rivera** owns the fictional boutique **Luna & Thread**. She photographs new arrivals on her phone, manages a small team, and cannot afford an enterprise virtual try-on implementation.

## MVP user journey

1. Elena applies from her phone.
2. An administrator approves the fictional boutique.
3. Elena adds a blazer and uploads a garment image.
4. TryOnReady identifies missing fields or source-image issues.
5. Elena corrects the product.
6. TryOnReady validates the product through YouCam Apparel VTO.
7. An administrator approves publication.
8. A consumer opens the product page, consents, and tries on the blazer.
9. Elena sees aggregated product activity without access to the consumer image.

## Required MVP features

- Responsive boutique application
- Application statuses
- Manual product entry
- Secure garment upload
- Digital-asset readiness checklist
- Real YouCam Apparel VTO integration
- Background job status
- Administrative approval
- Public product page
- Consumer consent and photo upload
- Private try-on result
- Aggregated boutique dashboard
- QR or share link
- iPhone and Android browser compatibility

## Judging alignment

### Technological implementation

A working, non-trivial YouCam Apparel VTO workflow with upload, task creation, status handling, result retrieval, failure handling, and secure server-side credentials.

### Design

A coherent boutique-to-consumer experience rather than an isolated API screen.

### Potential impact

Makes advanced retail technology more accessible to small boutiques without an enterprise development budget.

### Quality of idea

Treats YouCam as boutique-enablement infrastructure rather than another general styling chatbot.

## Success criteria

The MVP is ready when one end-to-end scenario works reliably on desktop and a phone-sized browser, automated tests pass, setup instructions are complete, and the demo can be explained in three minutes.

## Deferred features

- Native iOS and Android store packages
- Payments and subscriptions
- Live inventory feeds
- CSV catalog import
- Multi-retailer marketplace search
- Conversion attribution
- Return prediction
- Physical sizing or fit claims
- Automated production onboarding
